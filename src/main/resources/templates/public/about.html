About us

Built for modern builders

Floor21 helps real-estate builders run day-to-day sales and finance from one place — projects, flats, bookings, payment schedules, receipts, and team access.

Builder offices often juggle spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads, and paper files. Floor21 brings those workflows together so your team can track inventory, close bookings, follow payment milestones, and keep receipts and demand letters organised.

Whether you manage one project or several, Floor21 is designed for clear records, faster follow-ups, and less operational chaos.
