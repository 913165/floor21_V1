Privacy

How we handle your data

Floor21 is built for builder workspaces. Access stays with signed-in users for your organisation.

Account credentials and project data are used only to run Floor21 for your builder account — bookings, receipts, schedules, and related records your team creates in the product.

We do not sell your project data. If you have privacy questions or need help with account access, contact us using the details on the Contact page.
