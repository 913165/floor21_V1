EMAIL 1 — INITIAL INTRODUCTION (formal, full)

Subject: Simplify flat booking and milestone collections — Floor21

Dear [Name / Team],

I hope this message finds you well.

I am writing to introduce **Floor21** — a web-based platform designed specifically
for residential real estate developers and builders who manage multiple projects,
buildings, and milestone-based buyer payments.

Many teams we speak to still rely on spreadsheets, WhatsApp updates, and manual
follow-up to track which flats are available, which are booked, how much is due
per milestone, and whether demand letters and receipts have been issued. Floor21
brings these workflows into one secure system.

**What Floor21 helps you manage:**

• **Inventory** — Interactive flat grid by building and floor (available, booked,
  hold), with support for parking and ground-floor units

• **Sales** — Client records, bookings (including joint owners), brokers, and
  cancellations

• **Milestones & collections** — Payment schedules slab-wise, payment receipts,
  demand letter generation (Word export), and tracking of issued vs pending demand
  letters

• **Finance visibility** — Building-wise dashboard showing due till latest slab,
  amount received, and balance pending; flat-wise list of pending demand letters

• **Team access** — Builder admin and sales partner roles with building-level
  permissions; each developer’s data stays fully isolated

• **Optional secure Vault** — PIN-protected booking-wise income and expense
  tracking for authorized staff

Floor21 is built for how Indian developers work: instalment milestones tied to
construction stages, rupee-based ledgers, printable receipts, and clear payment
trails for allottees.

We would be glad to walk you through a short demo tailored to your project
structure — whether you manage one tower or multiple buildings under one company.
