Headline: Everything from inventory to instalments in one place

PAGE 4 — KEY FEATURES (use icons + short descriptions)

**Property & inventory**
- Multi-project, multi-building setup
- Interactive flat grid (available, booked, hold)
- Floor-wise layout, BHK types, parking and basement plans
- Ground-floor shops and parking slot linking

**Sales & CRM**
- Client master with KYC-style details
- Booking management with joint owners
- Broker tracking
- Booking cancellations with audit trail

**Milestones & collections**
- Central milestone templates per building
- Per-booking payment schedule with slab-wise ledger
- Payment receipts with GST/TDS handling
- Demand letter generation (Word export)
- Track demand letters issued vs pending on dashboard
- Allottee ledger for client-facing payment history

**Finance & control**
- Bank accounts (instalment vs GST accounts for demand letters)
- PIN-protected Vault for booking-wise income/expense (role-based access)
- Expense tracking for builder admins
- Printable receipts with legal company name from profile

**Dashboard & visibility**
- Building-wise payment summary: due till latest slab, received, pending
- Demand letter summary: total, issued, remaining
- Flat-wise list of pending demand letters
- Booking pipeline value at a glance

**Team & security**
- Multi-tenant: each builder’s data fully isolated
- Roles: Builder admin, sales partner (building-scoped access)
- Platform admin for onboarding multiple developers
- Secure login, session-based access
